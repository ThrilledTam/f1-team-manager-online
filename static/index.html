❄️ Winter break

Each winter-break round, every team offers up to 3 drivers (max 2 for main seats, max 1 for a Test & Reserve slot). Offers are not mandatory unless your lineup is not full. When every team hits NEXT, each driver signs with the best-placed constructors' team that offered them. After 3 rounds, any team still short of a driver gets one from the bot manager. Use the Driver Market tab to pick your offers, and the Research tab for last-minute regulation R&D.
