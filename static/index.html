🏎️ F1 Team Manager Online · v0 beta

A clean online rebuild of the PyCharm F1 team-manager game. Create a room, pick a team, manage it through the season — and race your friends at the same time.


Seasons: 2026 only in v0. 2027 / 2028 come later.

This game runs in a browser tab — there is nothing to close from inside the game. Just close this tab when you are done. 👋
